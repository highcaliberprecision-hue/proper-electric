01

load assessment

We calculate your home's actual electrical load — current and planned (EV, solar, hot tub) — to size the right panel, not just the biggest one.

02

ComEd coordination

Service upgrades usually require a scheduled outage with ComEd for the meter/service entrance work — we handle that coordination.

03

install day

Old panel comes out, new panel goes in, circuits get labeled and re-terminated, grounding brought up to current code.

04

permit & inspection

Panel upgrades require a permit and inspection in virtually every Chicagoland municipality — we file and coordinate it.
