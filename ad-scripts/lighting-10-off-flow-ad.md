## Tips that save credits

- **Never let Flow render text.** Veo garbles words, prices and URLs. The "10% OFF" and the phone number both go on in CapCut. That's why every prompt ends with "No text, no subtitles."
- **Keep dialogue under about 18 words per clip.** Veo speaks one 8-second clip at a time. Longer lines get rushed or cut off.
- **Her voice can change between clips.** Veo makes up a new voice for each clip. If clips 1–3 sound like different women, pick the take you like best and run the other clips through ElevenLabs Voice Changer (or re-record her lines with one voice) before editing.
- **Generate 2–4 takes per clip** and pick the best. Clip 3 usually needs the most re-rolls, mostly for lip sync.
- **If her face or suit drifts between takes,** check that the reference sheet is attached and that her description was pasted in full.
- **Want a real job in Clip 2?** You can't attach a person Ingredient and start/end frames together. If you'd rather reveal his real job (`assets/work/exterior-holiday-lighting.jpg`), make Clip 2 in Frames to Video without her, and put her voiceover over it in CapCut.

## Editing (CapCut)
1. Drop the clips in order 1 → 2 → 3, then add a 2-second end card on a black or navy background.
2. Burn in captions for every spoken line. Most people watch on mute.
3. Put a "10% OFF LIGHTING · CALL TO BOOK (847) 744-4625" banner in the top third during Clip 3, so the number is on screen while she says "call to book."
4. Export at 1080×1920, 30fps.
